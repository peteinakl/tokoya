A hot towel shave is $65 and takes 45 minutes. Hot towels, lather, a
straight-razor shave and a finishing massage.

### The ritual step by step
1. **Pores & Prep:** Steamed towels infused with eucalyptus and natural botanical oils wrap the face to open pores and soften coarse facial hair, followed by Proraso pre-shave cream to protect the skin barrier.
2. **Lather & Cushion:** Rich, warm shave soap is whipped and massaged into the beard with a traditional brush, lifting each hair for a close glide.
3. **Single-Blade Precision:** Using a fresh, surgical-grade blade in a traditional straight razor, your barber makes smooth, deliberate passes along the grain to avoid irritation and eliminate razor burn.
4. **Cold Compress & Relief:** A cold compress towel is applied to tighten pores and cool the skin, followed by alcohol-free aftershave balm and a relaxing shoulder and neck pressure-point massage.
