A men’s haircut at Tokoya is $50 and takes 30 minutes. It is the standard
cut at both shops: clippers or scissors through the sides, scissor work on
top, finished with a neck and outline tidy.

### What to expect
Every cut begins with a focused consultation to assess your hair texture, growth patterns, and everyday routine. Your barber works with clipper guards or scissor-over-comb through the sides, shapes and balances length on top, and cleans the perimeter with a foil shaver or straight razor. The service finishes with a warm neck wipe and professional styling using Layrite pomade, Uppercut Deluxe matte clay, or styling powder to lock in your preferred finish.

### Upkeep & frequency
To maintain a neat outline, clean taper, and balanced weight through the top, we recommend returning every 3 to 4 weeks. If you wear a tighter taper around the neck and ears, a quick outline tidy between appointments keeps your cut looking sharp.
